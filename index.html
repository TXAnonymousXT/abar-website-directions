New Direction 4

Model Coast

“We look after the green ones.”

The sales-gallery model, put on the website. Doha and Lusail are cast in white on a glossy charcoal sea, and every building ABAR manages is solid forest green. Visitors turn the model, fly to a district, drag the sun to sweep the shadows, and click a green building to see what’s available inside it. The same idea carries the owner pitch: “Make yours one of the green ones.”

New Direction 5

Pop-up

Homes of Qatar, unfolded.

A forest-green clothbound pop-up book on a charcoal desk. Scrolling turns the pages — they curl like real paper — and each chapter raises a district in paper cut-outs that cast real shadows: Katara Towers, Porto Arabia, Doha Tower, the Museum of Islamic Art, the villas of Al Wakrah. The ABAR building in every scene is printed green; the logo itself pops up on the title page.

Type
Young Serif, Figtree, DM Mono · Markazi Text Arabic
Signature
Scroll-driven 3D book with page curl and pop-ups
Mood
Crafted, warm, unforgettable

New Direction 6

Simple

A clean, bright site that just works.

A conventional, easy-to-scroll real estate site in ABAR’s colours and its own font, Poppins. Hero with search, featured properties with filters, the six popular areas, services, a landlord banner, the vision and contact — following the structure and wording of the current aba-r.com, with no 3D or artwork.
