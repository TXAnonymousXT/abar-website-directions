What we do

طرفان لكل عقد

Two sides of every lease

We work for the people who live in a home and the people who own it — and we only do rentals, so we do them carefully.

If you’re renting

Find it, sign it, settle in للمستأجرين

Tell us how you live and what you can spend. We come back with homes that fit, not a list of everything available.

  • Shortlists built around your commute, schools and budget
  • Viewings arranged at times that suit you, evenings included
  • Lease drafted, explained clause by clause, and registered
  • One number to call for maintenance after you move in
If you own property

Leased, kept, accounted for للملاك

We manage single apartments and whole buildings. You get a tenant, a monthly statement, and fewer phone calls.

  • Tenant screening, references and deposit handling
  • Rent collected and reported to you every month
  • Repairs and upkeep by contractors we’ve vetted
  • Renewals and vacancies planned before they cost you

Space for business

Offices, retail units and warehouses in Lusail, West Bay and the Industrial Area — leased on terms that suit a growing company.
